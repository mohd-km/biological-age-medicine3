Why Reference Ranges Are Not Targets

Population Normals vs. Low-Risk Physiology

Standard lab intervals cover the central 95% of an aging population. Inside those "normal" bounds, small upward shifts in glucose, RDW, hs-CRP, and white blood cell count compound exponentially in the Gompertz hazard. Medicine 3.0 treats those drifts years before clinical disease is diagnosed.

Model Attribution & Independent Validation

Levine et al. (2018) Formula & Real CDC NHANES Survival Check

Levine et al., Aging 2018; 10(4):573-591

Explicit model attribution: The PhenoAge Gompertz proportional-hazards formula and all ten coefficients below were derived and trained by Levine et al. (2018) (Aging 10(4):573-591, doi:10.18632/aging.101414) on NHANES III and validated by their group on NHANES IV. This application implements their exact published equations without modifying any coefficient, and adds Mohammed KM's own independent build-time survival validation on public CDC NHANES 1999-2010 microdata.

1. Linear Predictor (xb)
xb = -19.9067
   - 0.0336 * Albumin (g/L)
   + 0.0095 * Creatinine (umol/L)
   + 0.1953 * Glucose (mmol/L)
   + 0.0954 * ln(CRP in mg/dL)
   - 0.0120 * Lymphocyte (%)
   + 0.0268 * MCV (fL)
   + 0.3306 * RDW (%)
   + 0.00188 * ALP (U/L)
   + 0.0554 * WBC (10^3/uL)
   + 0.0804 * Age (years)
