Medicine 3.0 Clinical Framing

Why Population Reference Ranges Are Not Optimal Targets

Standard laboratory reference intervals (Medicine 2.0) typically span the central 95% of an aging, metabolically heterogeneous population. Within those "normal" bounds, small upward shifts in fasting glucose, RDW, hs-CRP, and leukocyte count compound exponentially in the Gompertz mortality hazard. Medicine 3.0 shifts the objective from late-stage disease detection to proactive risk-surface compression decades earlier.
